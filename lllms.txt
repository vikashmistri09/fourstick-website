## Growth Framework

Fourstick follows a four-stage performance marketing framework.

### 1. Strategy & Research

Before campaign launch, Fourstick evaluates:
- Target audiences
- Customer intent
- Competitors
- Market opportunities
- Offers
- Tracking requirements

### 2. Creative Production

Fourstick develops and tests:
- Static ads
- Video ads
- UGC
- Copy
- Creative concepts

The objective is to identify creative angles that improve CTR and conversions.

### 3. Launch & Optimization

Campaigns are launched across relevant advertising platforms and continuously optimized through:
- Audience testing
- Creative testing
- Landing page testing
- A/B testing
- Budget optimization
- Conversion tracking

### 4. Scale & Growth

Once campaigns demonstrate profitable performance, Fourstick scales budgets while monitoring efficiency, ROAS, acquisition costs, lead quality, and revenue.
